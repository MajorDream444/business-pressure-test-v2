# Anime.js Notes

## Status

Anime.js is planned for Sprint 2.

Do not install it during the foundation commit or Sprint 1 unless the sprint scope changes.

## Intended Use

Use Anime.js for the Animated Business MRI layer:

- question transitions
- progress meter
- score reveal
- leak map reveal
- CTA pulse

## Motion Rule

Motion serves clarity.

Avoid animation that slows the quiz or makes the diagnosis harder to read.

## Accessibility

Support `prefers-reduced-motion`.

Reduced-motion mode should keep every screen usable while removing large movement and count-up effects.
