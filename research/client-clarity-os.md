# Client Clarity OS Research

## Source Role

Client Clarity OS is the operating layer this product feeds.

Business Pressure Test V2 should become the front door into that system.

## Useful Doctrine

- Do not build before extracting clarity.
- Airtable is the operational source of truth.
- AI drafts; human approves.
- Separate diagnosis from proposal.
- Separate first build from long-term vision.
- Keep pricing and final terms human-reviewed.

## Product Translation

Business MRI should collect enough signal to recommend a next move without pretending to be the full Client Clarity OS.

The result should support:

- diagnosis
- blueprint recommendation
- MOBS routing
- eventual Airtable record creation

## Sprint Boundary

Sprint 1 is local-only and does not connect to Airtable, Notion, Stripe, CRM, or automation.
