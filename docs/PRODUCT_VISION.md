# Product Vision

## Project

Business Pressure Test V2

## Mission

Turn a static business quiz into an Animated Business MRI that diagnoses operational pressure and recommends the next useful build.

## Product Role

This product is the front door for Client Clarity OS.

It should convert a founder's answers into:

- a clear business pressure diagnosis
- a score
- a primary leak
- a recommended next move
- eventually, an internal MOBS routing decision

## Experience Standard

The experience should feel premium, direct, and useful before any sales conversation.

It should not feel like a generic lead magnet. It should feel like a diagnostic instrument.

## Principles

- Diagnose before selling.
- Ask only useful questions.
- Motion serves clarity.
- Every answer produces intelligence.
- Every score produces a recommendation.
- Airtable is the source of truth when integrations begin.
- MOBS routing is the internal intelligence layer.

## Current Product Boundary

Sprint 1 is local-only.

Do not add:

- Airtable
- authentication
- payment
- CRM
- automation
- dashboards

The next win is proving the diagnosis flow locally.
