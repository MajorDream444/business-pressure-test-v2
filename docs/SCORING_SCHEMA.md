# Scoring Schema

## Current Goal

Define a simple scoring model for Sprint 1 that can later support MOBS routing and Airtable capture.

## Score Range

Total score: `0` to `100`.

Each question should contribute to the total score. The exact question set can evolve during Sprint 1, but the output contract should remain stable.

## Recommended Bands

### 0-25: Critical

Pattern: the founder is the system.

Likely issues:

- business depends on daily founder effort
- lead flow is fragile
- follow-up is inconsistent
- operations are not structured

### 26-50: Leaking

Pattern: there is movement, but the business is unstable.

Likely issues:

- inconsistent lead flow
- unclear offer conversion
- loose backend process

### 51-75: Functional But Capped

Pattern: the business works, but it is hitting a ceiling.

Likely issues:

- limited growth channels
- incomplete automation
- under-optimized conversion path

### 76-100: Ready To Scale

Pattern: the foundation is strong enough for leverage.

Likely issues:

- scale path needs focus
- channel mix may be too narrow
- backend monetization may be underbuilt

## Sprint 1 Outputs

Sprint 1 should produce:

- total score
- diagnosis band
- short diagnosis summary
- pressure points
- next moves

Sprint 3 expands this into primary leak, secondary leak, MOBS routing, and blueprint recommendation.
