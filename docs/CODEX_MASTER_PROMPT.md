# Codex Master Prompt

## Project

Business Pressure Test V2

## Mission

Turn a static quiz into an Animated Business MRI.

## Role In The System

This repo is the front door for Client Clarity OS and the future Business MRI layer.

## Principles

- Diagnose before selling.
- Motion serves clarity.
- Every answer produces intelligence.
- Every score produces a recommendation.
- Airtable is source of truth.
- MOBS routing is internal intelligence layer.

## Current Goal

Build local MVP before backend integrations.

## Build

- Landing page
- Quiz
- Diagnosis
- Results

## Do Not

- Do not overengineer.
- Do not add authentication.
- Do not add subscriptions.
- Do not add dashboards.
- Do not add Airtable before Sprint 4.
- Do not add CRM before Sprint 4.
- Do not add payments before explicit approval.
- Do not add automation before explicit approval.

## Sprint Discipline

Sprint 1 proves the experience.

Sprint 2 adds Anime.js motion.

Sprint 3 adds business intelligence and MOBS routing.

Sprint 4 connects Airtable.

## Quality Bar

The product should be:

- clear
- fast
- diagnostic
- premium
- local-first until integrations are intentionally added

Every future change should ask:

- Does this improve the diagnosis?
- Does this keep scope aligned with the current sprint?
- Does this preserve the future Airtable and MOBS intelligence layer?
